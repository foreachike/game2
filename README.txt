Godot has been a bum for far too long, and soon he will pay the price (death)

Its up to you to save him by controlling his every move and making him remember how to do essential things like:

-EAT (right arrow to cook pizzas, down arrow while in the center to eat them)
-DRINK (hold the left arrow to drink soda because he has no water in his household)
-COMBAT BOREDOM (hold nothing to go to the center and listen to groovy tunes)

if you let him die all video games end forever
